Common questions

Questions we are often asked.

01How is this different from traditional leadership development programmes?+

This is not a leadership development programme. It is targeted work with the women your business already relies on, so they can operate at the level the role requires.

It is practitioner-led. The lens is not HR theory but frontline corporate reality: how promotion committees assess readiness, how power moves, and what senior stakeholders need to see before they back someone.

02What measurable outcomes do organisations typically see?+

Women moving into broader scope and senior roles. Stronger promotion cases. Greater visibility with senior stakeholders. Leaders who delegate and lead through others rather than carrying execution personally.

Success measures are agreed with your sponsor at the start, and the final review is against those outcomes, not satisfaction scores.

03How quickly does leadership behaviour start to shift?+

Visible change typically appears within three to six months. Because the work is applied inside live roles, between sessions and under real business pressure, sponsors usually notice the difference before the programme ends.

04Can this work across multiple countries or regions?+

Yes. Pascale has led global teams across regions and works with organisations across the UK and Europe. Programmes run in London, hybrid, or remotely, and coaching works across time zones.

05Who tends to benefit most from these interventions?+

High-performing women at Director, VP, and senior specialist level who are trusted to deliver but not yet seen as leaders, and organisations at a point of promotion, succession, or retention risk.

It is not for organisations looking for motivational talks, generic workshops, or low-friction interventions. This work requires willingness to look at what is actually happening, and act on it.
