Leadership Transition Programme

For women transitioning from high performance into strategic leadership.

A multi-week leadership transition programme for senior women moving beyond technical excellence and operational delivery into influence, visibility, and enterprise-level leadership.

Because the skills that create exceptional performers are not the same skills that create influential leaders.

This isn’t a leadership development programme. It’s a practitioner-led transition programme, designed by a senior leader who has made these moves herself, and who has enabled other women to do the same. The lens here isn’t HR. It’s frontline corporate reality.

These sessions are led by Pascale, a former senior leader in global financial organisations, who has navigated these transitions herself and designed this programme from lived corporate experience, not theory.

The issue is rarely the quality of these women’s thinking. Many are already among the strongest operators in the organisation. The problem is that their influence often does not match the value they bring.

When high-performing women strengthen visibility, strategic positioning, and stakeholder management, organisations benefit from stronger succession pipelines, broader leadership contribution, and better strategic decision-making.

  • Strengthen stakeholder management and upward influence.
  • Manage the psychological transition from expert identity to leadership identity.
  • Increase strategic contribution and influence across the organisation.

Structure

  • Facilitated leadership working sessions led by Pascale.
  • Applied leadership challenges between sessions.
  • Peer accountability and strategic discussion inside the cohort.
  • Sponsor check-ins aligned with business outcomes.

Executive coaching

1-2-1 Leadership Coaching

For women moving from trusted executor to influential leader.

Strategic authority. Composed leadership presence. Stronger decision-making under pressure.

One-to-one leadership coaching with Pascale isn’t just guided reflection. It is informed by two decades of senior leadership in organisations of the highest calibre, including the decisions, transitions, politics, and moments of uncertainty that no programme fully prepares you for.

Confidential, strategic coaching for women operating at or approaching senior leadership level.

Ongoing executive coaching grounded in the realities of corporate power, promotion, visibility, stakeholder management, and leadership transition. Real operating experience and commercial reality, applied to the situations your leaders face this quarter.

Many of these women already possess the judgement, resilience, and commercial intelligence organisations need at senior level. But when exceptional talent remains trapped in execution rather than influence, businesses underutilise some of their strongest leadership capability.

As visibility, stakeholder management, and strategic influence strengthen, organisations benefit from broader leadership contribution, stronger collaboration, and better decision-making at senior level.

What participants leave with

  • Greater influence across senior stakeholders and decision-makers.
  • Stronger political intelligence and organisational navigation.
  • The ability to lead through others rather than carrying execution personally.
  • More visible, credible leadership presence under pressure.
  • A confidential strategic space to think beyond day-to-day delivery.

Structure

  • Quarterly executive coaching engagement: 6 × 60-minute sessions per quarter.

Best fit

For organisations investing in high-performing women at moments of increased visibility, promotion, succession, or leadership transition, and unwilling to leave the outcome to chance.

Minimum 3 coachees per organisation

Strategic leadership simulation

Atlantis

You are about to discover what really happens inside leadership teams when certainty disappears.

A behavioural laboratory for senior teams and high-potential cohorts. Mixed-gender, and often the first way organisations work with us.

15–30 participants · Full day · Facilitated debrief

What organisations gain

  • Real-time visibility into how leaders actually operate under pressure.
  • A clearer view of how pressure, hierarchy, communication patterns, and workplace culture influence the way teams operate and make decisions.
  • Early identification of leadership potential, behavioural risks, and collaboration gaps.
  • A far more accurate picture of leadership readiness than interviews or workshops alone can provide.
  • A shared vocabulary for talent conversations and development priorities.

What participants leave with

  • Honest insight into their own behaviour under pressure.
  • Sharper decision-making when information is incomplete.
  • A first-hand understanding of how influence, alliances, and trust are built, or lost.
  • The experience of influencing without relying on hierarchy.

Best fit

High-potential cohorts, succession pipelines, executive offsites, and senior team development. For organisations that want to observe leadership capability in real time, not discuss it in theory.

Atlantis is one of the most advanced civilisations ever built. And it is starting to fracture.

Political tensions are rising. Critical systems are becoming unstable. Trust in leadership is eroding, and the factions inside Atlantis no longer agree on what should happen next.

Your people sit on the High Council. Each governs a domain the civilisation cannot survive without.

  • Energy
  • Infrastructure
  • Public Stability
  • Trade
  • Innovation
  • Defence
  • Knowledge
  • Resources
  • Diplomacy

Over the day, the crises escalate. Information is incomplete. Priorities compete. Some agendas are hidden. Ethical lines get tested, and every decision costs someone something.

Most organisations do not fail from lack of intelligence. The real challenge is whether people speak, influence, challenge, and decide when uncertainty rises. Leadership patterns emerge fast when the stakes feel real.

What people do under pressure reveals more than personality tests ever will.

What the room reveals

  • Who becomes influential
  • Who self-silences
  • Who creates trust
  • Who dominates
  • Who avoids conflict
  • Who thinks strategically
  • Who over-functions operationally
  • How alliances form
  • How fear shapes decisions
  • How power actually moves

Competence and influence are not the same thing. People rarely realise how power actually moves inside groups until they experience it. This is not role play or team-building. It is live behavioural observation.

The business case

Most organisations have already run the assessments, the interviews, and the performance reviews.

Atlantis complements and stress-tests that picture. It shows you how your high-potential leaders actually behave when the stakes are real, not how they say they do.

Structure

  • Participants form the High Council, each governing a domain of Atlantis.
  • Multiple decision rounds with escalating crises and evolving constraints.
  • Live behavioural observation by facilitators throughout.
  • Structured debrief connecting observed behaviour to workplace leadership.

After the behavioural laboratory

What happens after the day?

Atlantis does not end when the simulation ends.

The session surfaces angles, validates instincts, and identifies blind spots that standard assessments and leadership programmes rarely find. After the behavioural laboratory, we anchor those insights through a structured debrief, helping the organisation and participants translate what emerged in the room into clearer leadership behaviours, talent conversations, and next-step development priorities.

It gives your organisation a more accurate read on who your leaders truly are under pressure, and a shared vocabulary to act on it.
