Core insight

This is not a capability problem.

At senior level, progression is no longer determined solely by the quality of your work.

It is shaped by how you are perceived, how you position yourself, how you influence stakeholders, and how you operate inside complex organisational systems.

Most high-performing women are never taught this transition. They simply continue doing more of what made them successful earlier in their careers.

Leadership is not more execution.
It is influence, visibility, positioning, and strategic authority.
