About Pascale Lebaillif

Twenty years inside the rooms where senior decisions are made.

Two decades operating at the level your leaders are stepping into, and being assessed at.

Why this work

I’ve sat on the committees where careers are decided. I know exactly what separates those who step into leadership from those who remain valued for delivery.

It is not capability.
It is visibility, influence, and the ability to operate at the level the role demands.

I have watched ready, exceptional women passed over because no one had shown them how the senior game is played. For the business, the cost is concrete: a thin pipeline, promotion misfires, and senior roles filled by default rather than design.

Background

Both sides of the table.

Pascale Lebaillif spent over two decades in senior roles at Morgan Stanley, Credit Suisse, and J.P. Morgan, leading global teams, running complex programmes, and sitting on talent and promotion committees deciding senior progression.

The work is informed by being on both sides of the table: as the senior leader navigating power, and as the committee member assessing readiness. Direct, commercially grounded, and unsentimental about what senior leadership actually requires.

Today she works with organisations facing the same pattern: high-performing women not translating into senior leadership.
